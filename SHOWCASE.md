## Adding your project

1. Open a Show & Tell discussion:
   https://github.com/devituz/lagodev/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell
2. Fill in the template (project name, link, what it does, which
   lagodev parts you used).
3. Check the "showcase opt-in" box.
4. A maintainer will batch-update this file (we do it about once a
   month) — or you're welcome to send a PR adding your row to the
   table above.

## What we feature

We're looking for projects that:

- Have a public repo, a live URL, or a published article so others
  can learn from your setup.
- Use a non-trivial part of lagodev (more than "it imported the
  module") — the goal is to give newcomers concrete examples of how
  the framework fits together.
- Are still maintained. We periodically prune entries whose links
  404 or whose repos have been archived.

Closed-source, internal, or commercial projects are fine to mention
— a short description without a link still helps newcomers see the
range of use cases.
